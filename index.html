Free Deck Analysis — No Account, No Tracking

DeckLens runs entirely in your browser. Import via paste, file upload, or URL — get instant analysis with no data leaving your device.

MTG Features

Mana curve & color distribution, format legality checking, Deck DNA fingerprint, hypergeometric probability calculator, opening hand simulator, combo detection via Commander Spellbook, card synergy scores, Moxfield & Archidekt import, budget alternatives, and deck comparison.

Yu-Gi-Oh! Features

Level/rank charts, archetype detection, multi-banlist support for TCG, OCG, Master Duel, Goat Format, Edison, Speed Duel & Duel Links, opening hand calculator, test hand simulator with brick tracking, YDK/YDKE/Omega import, deck comparison, and tournament PDF export.

Privacy by Design

Zero backend, zero tracking, zero cookies. All analysis runs client-side in your browser. Your deck data never leaves your device. Works offline after first load. Open source and free forever.
