Azorius Control — Primer

This is a permission-based control deck built around Grand Arbiter Augustin IV as the commander. The primary gameplan is to establish an early tax advantage, then transition into a hard-lock win condition through stax pieces and card advantage engines.

Key Lines

Isochron Scepter + Dramatic Reversal: Infinite mana with 3+ mana rocks. Outlet through Walking Ballista or Blue Sun's Zenith.

Approach of the Second Sun: Cast, then dig with Dig Through Time or Mystic Remora for the re-cast. Backup wincon.

Matchup Notes

Strong against creature-heavy decks (wraths, pillowfort). Weak to fast combo decks that go under our tax pieces. Mulligan aggressively for interaction vs Turbo Naus.
